Name of Quantlet: Right_Tail_Portfolio

Published in: Measuring Statistical Risk

Description: This Python notebook estimates the right tail index of the German stock portfolio losses with a log-log regression on the largest order statistics and evaluates the regression with an analysis of variance. The resulting estimate is compared with the shape parameters obtained from a Generalized Extreme Value fit, a Generalized Pareto fit and the Hill estimator.

Keywords: tail index, regression, Hill estimator, extreme value index, portfolio
