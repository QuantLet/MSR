Name of Quantlet: Critical_values

Published in: Measuring Statistical Risk

Description: This Python notebook calibrates by simulation the critical values of the sequential testing procedure that selects the interval of homogeneity for a six-dimensional Clayton copula. For a geometric grid of nested window lengths it repeatedly simulates copula samples, estimates the dependence parameter by maximum likelihood and reports the resulting critical values for several risk levels and values of the parameter under the null hypothesis.

Keywords: critical values, Clayton copula, local parametric approach, maximum likelihood, Monte Carlo simulation
