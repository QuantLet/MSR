Published in: Measuring Statistical Risk

Description: This Python notebook examines whether the daily log returns of the German stock portfolio follow a normal distribution. It constructs a probability-probability plot and a quantile-quantile plot against the fitted normal distribution, which reveal the fat tails of the portfolio returns.
