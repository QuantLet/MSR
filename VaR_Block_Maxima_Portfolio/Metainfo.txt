Name of Quantlet: VaR_Block_Maxima_Portfolio

Published in: Measuring Statistical Risk

Description: This Python notebook estimates the daily Value-at-Risk of the German stock portfolio with the block maxima method, fitting a Generalized Extreme Value distribution within a rolling window. The estimates at the 95% and 99% confidence levels are backtested against the realised losses and the evolution of the fitted parameters over time is visualised.

Keywords: Value-at-Risk, block maxima, Generalized Extreme Value distribution, backtesting, rolling window
