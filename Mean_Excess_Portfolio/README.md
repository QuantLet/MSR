Name of Quantlet: Mean_Excess_Portfolio

Published in: Measuring Statistical Risk

Description: This Python notebook computes the empirical mean excess function of the largest losses of a portfolio of Bayer, BMW and Siemens stocks and compares it with the theoretical mean excess functions implied by a fitted Generalized Pareto distribution and by a Pareto model based on the Hill estimator. The comparison serves as a graphical diagnostic of the heaviness of the portfolio's loss tail.
