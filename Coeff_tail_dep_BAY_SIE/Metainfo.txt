Name of Quantlet: Coeff_tail_dep_BAY_SIE

Published in: Measuring Statistical Risk

Description: This Python notebook estimates the tail dependence between Bayer and Siemens stock returns by fitting Gaussian, Student-t, Gumbel, Clayton and Frank copulas to their standardised log returns and computing the implied tail dependence coefficients, complemented by a nonparametric estimator. The fitted copulas are then used in a rolling Monte Carlo backtest that compares the Value-at-Risk violation rates of the different dependence models.

Keywords: tail dependence, copula, Bayer, Siemens, Value-at-Risk
