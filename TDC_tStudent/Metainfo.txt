Name of Quantlet: TDC_tStudent

Published in: Measuring Statistical Risk

Description: This Python notebook computes the tail dependence coefficient of the Student-t copula as a joint function of the correlation and the degrees of freedom and displays it as a three-dimensional surface. The plot shows that tail dependence increases with the correlation and vanishes as the degrees of freedom grow large.
