Name of Quantlet: BMW_VOW_VaR_cop_GARCH_backtesting

Published in: Measuring Statistical Risk

Description: This Python notebook backtests the daily Value-at-Risk of a portfolio consisting of BMW and Volkswagen stocks using a copula-GARCH approach. On each day of a rolling window it fits GARCH(1,1) models to both assets, links their standardised residuals through a Clayton copula and simulates the portfolio profit and loss by Monte Carlo to obtain the Value-at-Risk, which is then compared with the realised outcomes to evaluate the accuracy of the model.

Keywords: Value-at-Risk, Clayton copula, GARCH, backtesting, Monte Carlo
