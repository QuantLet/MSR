Name of Quantlet: Maxima_POT

Published in: Measuring Statistical Risk

Description: This Python notebook illustrates the two classical sampling schemes of extreme value theory on a simulated series of losses. It shows how extreme observations are selected under the block maxima approach, where the largest loss within each block is retained, and under the peaks-over-threshold approach, where all losses above a given threshold are considered.

Keywords: block maxima, peaks-over-threshold, threshold, losses, extreme value theory
