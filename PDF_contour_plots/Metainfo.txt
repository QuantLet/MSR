Name of Quantlet: PDF_contour_plots

Published in: Measuring Statistical Risk

Description: This Python notebook plots the contour lines of four bivariate densities that share standard normal margins but are coupled by different copulas, namely the Gaussian, the Ali-Mikhail-Haq, the Frank and the Gumbel copula. The panels show how the choice of the copula alone reshapes the joint density even when the marginal distributions are identical.

Keywords: copula density, contour plot, Archimedean copula, normal margins, Sklar's theorem
