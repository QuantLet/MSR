Name of Quantlet: PP_Gumbel_vs_theoretical_Gumbel

Published in: Measuring Statistical Risk

Description: This Python notebook simulates a sample from the Gumbel distribution and constructs a probability-probability plot against the theoretical Gumbel distribution itself. Since the reference distribution matches the one that generated the data, the points lie close to the diagonal, illustrating what a good fit looks like.
