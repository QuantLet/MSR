Description: This Python notebook evaluates the density of the Student-t copula on a grid of the unit square and displays it as a three-dimensional surface. With few degrees of freedom the density exhibits pronounced peaks in both corners, reflecting the symmetric tail dependence that distinguishes the t copula from the Gaussian one.
