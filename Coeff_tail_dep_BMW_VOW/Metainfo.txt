Name of Quantlet: Coeff_tail_dep_BMW_VOW

Published in: Measuring Statistical Risk

Description: This Python notebook estimates the tail dependence between BMW and Volkswagen stock returns by fitting Gaussian, Student-t, Gumbel, Clayton and Frank copulas to their standardised log returns and computing the implied tail dependence coefficients, complemented by a nonparametric estimator. A rolling Monte Carlo backtest compares the Value-at-Risk violation rates of the copula models, and a figure contrasts the Value-at-Risk obtained from the Gumbel copula with the one obtained from the normal distribution.

Keywords: tail dependence, copula, BMW, Volkswagen, Value-at-Risk
